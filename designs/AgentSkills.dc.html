# When to use
Any list, table or feed that can be empty, slow or fail.

# Steps
1. Duplicate the board as "{name} · Empty" and "{name} · Error".
2. Empty: insert EmptyState variant=list. One sentence, one action.
3. Error: insert Banner tone=danger with a Retry action.
   Hide stale rows; never show data next to an error.
4. Copy follows the Acme voice: no "Oops", no exclamation marks.
5. Render both boards at 320 and 390 px and fix anything that wraps.

# Never
- Add new colors or detach tokens.
- Touch the tab bar or navigation.
